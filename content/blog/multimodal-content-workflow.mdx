# How to Build a Complete AI Content Workflow From Text to Video to Voice to Distribution in 6 Steps

Here's how to build a complete AI content workflow from text to video to voice to distribution in 6 steps, turning one blog idea into video, podcast clips, and social posts.

## How do you connect text, video, and voice AI into one workflow?

A unified AI content workflow integrates multiple generative AI technologies into a cohesive system. You begin with language models like ChatGPT or Claude to generate scripts, feed those scripts into AI video generators to create visual content, add synthetic narration through voice synthesis platforms, and finalize everything in a collaborative editing environment. This approach allows you to transform a single foundational piece of content—like a blog post—into polished videos, audio narratives, and social media assets while maintaining consistent messaging and brand identity throughout.

The workflow operates in a logical sequence but remains adaptable. You have the flexibility to bypass steps that don't align with your content strategy. A podcast-focused creator might skip video generation altogether. Someone prioritizing social media distribution could eliminate the blog phase. The underlying framework remains consistent regardless of which outputs you pursue.

## Your 6-step AI content workflow

1. **Write your script using Claude or ChatGPT.** Access Claude or ChatGPT and use a prompt like: *"Create a 90-second educational script about [your topic] that includes dialogue, visual directions for each scene, and suggestions for supporting B-roll footage. Break this into 3-5 concise segments, with each segment being 2-3 sentences."* Export this as a plain text document. Expected duration: 5 minutes.

2. **Generate video using available AI video tools.** Access AI video generation platforms such as Google AI Studio, YouTube Create, or comparable services. Input each scene description separately as an individual prompt to the video generator. These platforms produce variable quality outputs, with many offering built-in audio capabilities. Create multiple variations for each scene to have options during editing. Expected duration: 8-12 minutes (allowing 2-5 minutes per generated video segment).

3. **Create a professional voiceover with ElevenLabs or Murf.** Paste your script into one of these voice synthesis platforms. Choose a consistent speaker identity—using the same AI voice across your content library helps audiences recognize your brand across different media types and languages. Export the complete narration as an audio file. Expected duration: 3 minutes.

4. **Assemble in Descript.** Launch Descript and upload your video segments in sequential order. Add your audio narration as a separate track. Take advantage of Descript's transcript-based editing workflow to refine pacing and synchronization—you can edit the transcript directly to remove verbal hesitations, adjust timing, and generate captions automatically. Expected duration: 15-20 minutes.

5. **Export and format for distribution.** Save your finished video in multiple formats optimized for different platforms: 1920x1080 for YouTube and embedded blog content, square format (1:1) for [Instagram](/ai-instagram-post-generator) Reels, and vertical format (9:16) for [TikTok](/ai-tiktok-caption-generator) and YouTube Shorts. Descript's export function manages these conversions. Expected duration: 5 minutes.

6. **Distribute across channels.** Share the YouTube-optimized version on your blog as supplementary media. Use Descript's built-in clipping tools to extract shorter segments (around 15 seconds) suitable for social sharing. Transform the voiceover track into a standalone podcast episode. Leverage automation platforms like Zapier to schedule simultaneous posting across multiple social networks and email lists. Expected duration: 10 minutes.

**Total time: 50-55 minutes.** This compares favorably to conventional video production workflows, which typically require 8-16 hours when accounting for scriptwriting, filming, audio recording, and post-production editing.

## Choosing the right video generation tool

The landscape of AI video creation tools continues to expand, with numerous options now available to creators. When evaluating which platform fits your needs, assess output quality and resolution, whether the tool can generate or integrate audio, how well it connects with your other software applications, and the learning curve for new users. Take time to test different platforms' current offerings and examine their pricing models before integrating one into your production system.

## Real output: one blog post becomes multiple formats

A 1,500-word blog post on "How AI speeds up video production" becomes:

- **A 90-second YouTube explainer video** with AI-generated visuals, voice narration, and captions
- **A 15-minute podcast episode** (repurposed voiceover + background music from Adobe Firefly or similar)
- **Four 15-second TikTok clips** (using editing tools to highlight key moments)
- **A [LinkedIn](/ai-linkedin-post-generator) carousel** (key points extracted to slide deck)

All use the same voice, messaging, and style. No rewriting. No re-recording.
