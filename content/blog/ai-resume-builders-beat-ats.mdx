**Teal wins for career changers who need deep job matching. Jobscan wins for pure ATS keyword scoring. Rezi is the best all-in-one AI resume builder for most job seekers. LinkedIn Premium's AI rewriter is the easiest entry point if you're already there.**

## What ATS systems actually parse — and what that means for your resume

**Workday, Greenhouse, Lever, and iCIMS parse resumes into structured fields: job titles, dates, skills, education. They match those fields against job requirements using keyword and semantic scoring. Formatting that breaks parsing — tables, headers/footers, text boxes — can cause entire sections to disappear before a recruiter sees your file.**

Plain text or simple single-column layouts survive parsing best. [Our look at AI resume screening](/blog/ai-resume-screening-bias-risks) covers how recruiters see the output — this is the candidate side of the same problem.

## How each AI resume builder actually performs

**Rezi uses real-time ATS scoring and an AI content generator that rewrites bullet points to match job descriptions. Jobscan's keyword match score is the most transparent: it shows exactly which terms are missing versus the job posting. Teal's job tracker + tailoring combo is unmatched for managing multiple applications.**

Enhancv and Kickresume lead on design but trail on ATS optimization — useful when a human sees your resume first (referrals, creative roles). LinkedIn's AI rewriter is convenient but generic. Indeed's suggestions are minimal.

According to [Jobscan's research](https://www.jobscan.co/blog/fortune-500-use-applicant-tracking-systems/), 99% of Fortune 500 companies use ATS. Getting past it isn't optional.

## How to use ChatGPT or Claude to tailor any resume for free

No paid tool required. This three-step prompt structure works:

1. **Paste the job description** and say: "Extract the 10 most important skills and qualifications from this job description."
2. **Paste your current resume bullets** and say: "Rewrite these bullets to reflect [extracted skills], keeping each under two lines and quantified where possible."
3. **Ask for a gap check:** "What requirements in the job description are not addressed in my resume? How should I handle them?"

The key word: *handle* — not fake. If the gap is real, address it honestly in a cover letter.

## Why keyword stuffing backfires — and what actually works

**Over-stuffed keywords trigger spam filters in Workday and Greenhouse and read as incoherent to human reviewers. Industry analyses consistently show that excessive keyword density correlates with lower recruiter ratings — not higher.** The tools that work best — Rezi, Teal, Jobscan — flag *missing* keywords, not a target count to hit.

The human-in-loop approach: let the AI identify gaps, then rewrite yourself. AI finds what's missing; you add the context that makes it true.

## FAQ

### Which AI resume builder is best for entry-level job seekers?

Rezi's free tier covers three resumes with ATS scoring — enough for a focused search. Kickresume is a good second option for students who want design-forward templates with AI content help.

### Does Jobscan work with LinkedIn job postings?

Yes. You can paste a LinkedIn job URL or the job description text directly into Jobscan. It then scores your uploaded resume against that specific posting.

### What's the free tier on Teal?

Teal's free plan gives you the job tracker and basic resume builder. AI-powered tailoring features — the keyword matching and bullet rewriting — require the paid plan at $29/month.

### Can AI resume tools guarantee I pass ATS screening?

No. They improve keyword alignment and fix formatting issues that break parsing. But ATS systems also score for genuine fit — experience, titles, tenure. No AI resume builder can manufacture qualifications you don't have.

### Is LinkedIn Premium's AI rewriter worth it just for resume help?

Probably not on its own. LinkedIn Premium costs around $40/month and the AI rewriting is a small feature. If you're already paying for Premium for InMail and recruiter insights, it's a useful bonus — not a standalone reason to subscribe.
